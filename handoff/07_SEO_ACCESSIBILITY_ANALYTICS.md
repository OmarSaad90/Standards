# SEO, Accessibility, Analytics

## SEO

Required:
- SSR/SSG standard pages
- canonical URLs
- unique title
- unique description
- Open Graph
- sitemap.xml
- robots.txt
- subject pages
- grade pages
- real 404
- internal linking

Recommended title:
`{CODE} — {Label} | Aedifica Standards Explorer`

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA.

Required:
- keyboard navigation
- visible focus
- form labels
- semantic headings
- landmarks
- live result announcements
- reduced motion
- responsive reflow
- usable zoom
- contrast

Do not claim conformance until independently audited.

## Analytics

Recommended:
- standards_search
- standards_filter_change
- standard_open
- source_link_open
- subject_open
- grade_open

Do not send protected data.
